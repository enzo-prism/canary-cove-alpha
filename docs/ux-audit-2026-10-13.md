# Canary Cove design and UX audit — October 13, 2026

The audit preserves the Sand, reef, and canary design system and improves the guest journey from discovery through a stay inquiry. The source baseline was `f9a60d8` on `main`, verified against the Vercel production deployment and both canonical domains.

## Guest journey and findings

1. **Discover the estate.** The photography and typography provide a clear sense of place. The hero's absolutely positioned copy could outgrow short windows. The copy now contributes to the hero's height. Automatic photography has an accessible pause control, freezes its zoom, stops off-screen/in background tabs, and advances only to loaded images. Amenities/activity/location tickers and decorative reef loops have independent pause controls and respond to changed reduced-motion preferences.
2. **Navigate and search.** Desktop dropdowns closed on every Tab; hover could consume the first chevron click. Keyboard traversal now reaches every link and resumes the header order. Menus close when crossing the desktop breakpoint and restore focus to a visible control. Search restores the actual opener after its exit animation; recents and questions share a scrollable area in short windows. Recent-search removal and answer links provide 44px tap targets. Long smooth anchor jumps align again after arrival so responsive filmstrips and the shrinking header cannot leave the destination too low.
3. **Explore photos and films.** Off-screen slides exposed invisible keyboard targets. Shared carousels now make them inert and hide them from assistive technology, while keeping meaningful partial previews operable. Estate/testimonial/Stay pagination, lightbox arrows, review filter controls, and rate filters use 44px targets. The rate-month selector uses four columns below 360px so month buttons remain comfortably wide. The Stay gallery control row wraps on narrow phones. Lightboxes offer tap arrows on phones and restore the opener on close. Film controls remain visible while focused. Desktop filmstrips use native scrolling for touch/reduced motion; the outdoor strip pins only if its full measured contents fit. Explicit zero-minimum mobile grid tracks prevent Safari's intrinsic media sizing from clipping the Stay amenities and Adventures boat sections.
4. **Choose dates and request a stay.** Custom validation previously let past dates and fractional/exponent adult counts through. Dates now use the Belize day, validate real dates, and preserve the entered calendar date in every traveler timezone. Review details include years and wrap full values. Required choices stay selected, visited step targets stay at least 44px wide at 320px, controls lock during submission, and the success dialog scrolls in short windows. An unsupported date year yields a validation error instead of crashing rendering.
5. **Ask a question and read guest notes.** Contact errors are associated with the message field, and starting another inquiry restores heading focus. Changing review notes resets the note body to the top. Availability days have accessible date/status names. Main House pricing stacks as a ledger on narrow phones.

## Verification coverage

- All 13 local public routes, including Gallery, plus the public internal route/anchor inventory.
- Widths 320, 360, 390, 640, 768, 1023, 1024, 1280, 1440, and 1920px; portrait/tablet/desktop heights, plus 320–375px landscape-height dialogs and navigation.
- Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit release coverage; mouse/keyboard/swipe/wheel, breakpoint transitions, reduced motion, and the normal Lenis scrolling path.
- Mocked form success/failure, in-flight state, timezone rollover, long review values, invalid dates/counts, and optional availability failure. Synthetic tests never submit real leads.
- Production build, typecheck, lint, unit tests, and intentional Chromium visual baseline review.
- Enabled buttons visible in default public-page states have their hit boxes measured at 320px and 390px. Representative interactive and motion states have separate checks. Dropdown Tab handling is consistent in Safari; page-link traversal tests use Safari's native Option+Tab behavior outside the disclosure.
- The three changed Darwin Chromium visual baselines are the mobile/desktop homepage hero (pause control) and Stay mini gallery (44px pagination). Linux baselines require the corresponding Linux runner refresh.

The regression additions live in `e2e/audit-responsive.spec.ts`, `e2e/navigation-accessibility.spec.ts`, `e2e/form-edge-cases.spec.ts`, `e2e/media-accessibility.spec.ts`, `e2e/motion-accessibility.spec.ts`, and `e2e/smooth-scroll.spec.ts`.

## Practical limits

The browser matrix emulates devices; it does not establish perfection on every physical device, assistive technology, or browser version. The separate Main House microsite remains an external destination. Live Formspree delivery and lead-dashboard operations are outside this synthetic UX pass. The private guest guide still awaits its approved content. Production availability remains contingent on the existing calendar integration; its failure fallback is tested.
